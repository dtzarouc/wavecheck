# Published source for the M1 reproduction

D. C. Tzarouchis, P. Ylä-Oijala and A. Sihvola, "Unveiling the scattering behavior of small
spheres", Phys. Rev. B 94, 140301(R) (2016), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.94.140301; arXiv:1606.05523v4.

No digitised data points are used. The equations taken from the paper (Eqs. 7, 8, 9, 12, 13, 14,
15) are transcribed in `src/wavecheck/physics/quasistatic.py` with their equation numbers.
